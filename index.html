ABOUT THE ARENA

Free browser games for curious minds.

SHAHEEN DAILY ARENA brings together short online games that run in a modern mobile or desktop browser. Pick a quick memory puzzle, practise number patterns, solve word clues, test your reactions or play a strategy board against a local AI opponent.

How do Coins and S-Coins work?

Coins are earned by completing rounds. S-Coins are special free rewards for boss victories and the daily variety mission. Use S-Coins to unlock visual themes in the Premium Vault; they have no cash value.

Is this real multiplayer?

No. This edition is single-player. Tic-Tac-Toe and Connect Four use local game logic, while the themed bosses are puzzle challenges. It does not display fake players or pretend that a server is online.
