How SHAHEEN DAILY ARENA Works

Play short challenges, learn from each attempt and build a record of your progress over time.

1. Choose a game

Choose from memory, endless maths, typing, rotating knowledge questions, number patterns, word scrambles, true/false facts and number comparisons. Generated games create fresh rounds while you play.

2. Complete the challenge

Follow the instructions shown inside the game. Your score is based on the rules of that activity, such as correct answers, matching pairs or typing accuracy.

3. Track your progress

Your points and completed-game count are saved in this browser using local storage. Clearing browser data or changing devices can reset or separate your progress.

4. Return for another challenge

Try a different activity, improve your best result and return to the rotating question bank. Progress stays in this browser and can be backed up as a JSON file.

Why play brain and skill games?

Short puzzles and practice activities can make learning feel more interactive. Memory matching encourages attention to card positions, maths questions provide arithmetic practice, typing tests give feedback on speed and accuracy, and quizzes help you review general knowledge. These games are for entertainment and practice; they are not medical or educational assessments.

Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Useful details about playing games and saving progress.

Are the games free to play?

Yes. The games included on this page are free to play in your web browser. No payment or account is required.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Open this website in a modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer and select a game.

Does SHAHEEN DAILY ARENA save my score?

Progress is saved in this browser on this device. Export a backup to move it to another device; automatic cross-device sync needs a secure account and database service, which is not part of this static website.

How does the typing speed test work?

The typing test runs for 30 seconds. It estimates words per minute from the number of characters typed and shows an accuracy estimate against the displayed sample passage.

Will the daily quiz change?

The quiz rotates through the included question bank and reshuffles it when a full cycle is complete. Generated maths and number-pattern games create fresh questions during play.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. The layout and controls adjust for mobile screens. A current browser with JavaScript enabled is required.
